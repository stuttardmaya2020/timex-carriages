The company

Executive travel in London, handled from the first call to the final mile.

TimeX Carriages is a London executive chauffeur service built on one simple promise. You will never have to wonder whether your car is coming.

Founder Azmat looks after every booking personally. Flights are tracked, routes are planned around the traffic, and your chauffeur arrives early, dressed for the occasion, in an immaculate car. You just get in.

Whether it's a board meeting in the City, an early flight from Gatwick or an evening in the West End, the standard is the same.

Precision

Punctual to the minute. Routes, timings and flights are checked before every journey.

Discretion

Quiet, professional chauffeurs. Your conversations and your movements stay private.

Excellence

Late-model Mercedes-Benz and BMW cars, kept spotless and driven with care.

Services

Wherever the day takes you, we'll be there.

  • AD

    As directed

    A car and chauffeur at your disposal by the hour. Change plans as you go and your driver adapts.

  • AIR

    Airport transfers

    Heathrow, Gatwick, City, Stansted, Luton and private terminals. Flights tracked, meet and greet at arrivals.

  • CORP

    Corporate travel

    Roadshows, client visits and account travel for teams that need to arrive composed and on time.

  • EVT

    Special events

    Weddings, galas, premieres and race days, with cars and chauffeurs prepared for the occasion.

  • P2P

    Pick-ups and drop-offs

    Point-to-point journeys across London and beyond, for meetings, hotels, stations and home.
